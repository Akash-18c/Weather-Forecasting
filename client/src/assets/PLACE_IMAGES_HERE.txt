IMPORTANT: Place Your Images Here
===================================

Please copy your image files to this folder:
d:\Weather Forecasting Web\client\src\assets\

Required files:
1. Sun.png - Your sun image for day mode
2. moon.webp - Your moon image for night mode

The images will be used in:
- Navbar: Day/Night toggle button (shows sun when dark mode is ON, moon when OFF)
- Weather Card: Right side next to temperature (shows sun during day, moon during night based on API data)

Features added:
✅ Custom sun/moon images in navbar toggle
✅ Custom sun/moon images in weather card based on time of day
✅ Floating animation for both images
✅ Gentle rotation for moon
✅ Glowing effect for sun
✅ Smooth transitions

After placing the images, the app will automatically use them!
